Biodex

The dex of living things. Photograph whatever you come across, fill your collection, compare it with your friends'.

Download the Android app iPhone: add the site to your home screen Source on GitHub Host your own server

How it works

A photo goes to your server. A detector frames the animal, a vision model compares it to nearly a million species, and the matching slot fills up in your dex. Plants and fungi have their own engine. Nothing leaves your server.

📸 Photograph

From the Android app, or from the website — on iPhone there is no app and none is needed: add the site to the home screen. Crop, cut out with your finger, pick the kingdom, send.

🧠 Recognise

MegaDetector to frame, BioCLIP 2 to name, Pl@ntNet for plants. A frequency prior rules out improbable exotic species.

📚 Collect

976,000 slots, five rarity levels, 50 ranks, 82 achievements with their own 3D frog. Arrange your dex like a desktop.

👥 Share

Friends, activity feed, reactions, leaderboard, map of everyone's finds. You decide who sees what.

🎨 Dress up

Seventeen themes, thirteen with an animated backdrop: falling glyphs, northern lights, savanna, nebula…

🔒 Keep

No email, no tracking, no central server. Your photos stay with you, between you and your friends.

Host your own server

A Linux box with Python 3.12, 8 GB of RAM and about twenty gigabytes of disk is enough. No GPU needed: an analysis takes about fifteen seconds on a CPU.

git clone https://github.com/Raaatus/BioDex.git
cd BioDex/backend
uv venv .venv && uv pip install -p .venv/bin/python -r requirements.txt -r requirements-moteur.txt
# catalogues and weights: see the README, section “The data”
./run.sh                      # the API, on :8077
.venv/bin/python serveur_web.py   # the web client, on :8090

The repository README details every step: the data to download, the systemd service, HTTPS, upgrading without losing anything.
